01 / Approach

Rigorous enough to trust.
Practical enough to use.

  1. 01

    Frame the real question

    Begin with context. Separate the visible symptom from the deeper question, then define what a useful answer would make possible.

  2. 02

    Make evidence tangible

    Use focused research and small experiments to turn assumptions into things we can inspect, challenge, and improve.

  3. 03

    Leave a clear trail

    Share the reasoning, limits, and practical implications so the work can travel beyond the room where it began.
